What we do

Build the structure growth requires.

Genysis IQ works at the intersection of strategy, operations, leadership systems, sales execution and practical artificial intelligence.

01

Business scaling & architecture

We identify why growth is slowing or creating chaos, then design the organizational architecture required to support the next stage.

  • Growth bottleneck diagnosis
  • Owner-dependency reduction
  • Organizational structure and role clarity
  • Decision rights and accountability
  • Management cadence and operating rhythm
02

Systems, processes & SOPs

A business cannot scale consistently when its best practices live inside individual employees’ heads. We convert execution into repeatable systems.

  • Process mapping and redesign
  • SOP architecture
  • Workflow and handoff improvement
  • KPI and scorecard design
  • Onboarding and training systems
03

Sales & customer growth

Growth is not only about attracting leads. It requires a consistent sales process, a stronger customer experience and a team equipped to convert opportunity.

  • Sales-process design
  • Staff sales and consultative selling training
  • Lead handling and follow-up systems
  • Customer journey improvement
  • Referral and retention strategy
04

Practical AI strategy

We help companies use AI as a business tool, not as a science experiment. Our focus is selecting and integrating third-party technology that solves real operating problems.

  • AI opportunity assessment
  • Penetration testing and security assessment
  • Inbound and after-hours conversational AI
  • Workflow automation strategy
  • AI-enabled marketing and customer service
  • Vendor evaluation and implementation guidance
05

Digital presence & discoverability

Your website and digital footprint should communicate authority, convert prospects and make the company discoverable in both traditional search and AI-powered search.

  • Website strategy and conversion improvement
  • SEO and local search optimization
  • AI search discoverability
  • Content architecture
  • Google Business Profile strategy
