Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Genysis IQ LLC

CardLinq is a digital business card app made by Genysis IQ LLC (“we”, “us”). It is designed so that your information stays on your device. Apart from the optional Apple Wallet feature described below, CardLinq does not communicate with any server, and we do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.

Information you enter

The cards you create (name, job title, contact details, photos, logos, links and networking notes) and the contacts you save are stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s private storage. They are included in your device’s normal iCloud or computer backups. Genysis IQ cannot access them.

Sharing

Your card leaves your device only when you choose to share it: when someone scans your QR code, or when you send your contact card using the iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages or Mail). You control which fields appear on each card.

Camera, Photos and Contacts

  • Camera is used only to scan QR codes while you are on the Scan screen. Images are not recorded or stored.
  • Photos are used only when you pick a profile photo, a logo or an image to scan for a QR code. CardLinq receives only the photos you choose.
  • Contacts are used only when you tap “Add to iPhone Contacts”. CardLinq does not read your address book.

Home Screen widget

To show your QR code in the widget, the app keeps a copy of your card’s QR data in storage shared only between CardLinq and its widget, on your device.

Apple Wallet

If you tap “Add to Apple Wallet”, the details shown on that card (name, job title, company, email, phone, website, photo and QR code) are sent over an encrypted connection to our pass-signing service. Apple requires every pass to be signed this way. The service creates the pass, returns it to your iPhone and immediately discards your details. They are not stored, logged or used for any other purpose. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button.

Analytics and tracking

CardLinq contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. This website does not set cookies.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase All Data in the app, or delete the app, to remove everything CardLinq has stored. Remove any Wallet passes from the Wallet app.

Children

CardLinq is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Questions about privacy: info@genysisiq.com.
