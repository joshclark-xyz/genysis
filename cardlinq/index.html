Features

Everything a business card should be.

Make the card once, then share it however suits the moment.

Design your card

Add your photo and company logo, then pick from six themes, two layouts and four fonts. Hide any field with one tap without deleting it.

Share with a QR code

Anyone can point their phone camera at your code and save your contact. They don’t need CardLinq.

Home Screen widget

Put your QR code on your Home Screen in small, medium or large, so it’s ready without opening the app.

Apple Wallet pass

Add your card to Apple Wallet and show your QR code straight from Wallet, even from the Lock Screen.

Grow your network

Tell people who you want to meet with “A Good Professional Connection for Me Is…” and “A Good Customer Referral for Me Is…”.

Multiple cards

Keep a separate card for each role or business. Duplicate one to make variations quickly.

Scan and save

Scan someone’s QR code with your camera or from a photo or screenshot. Save it in CardLinq or add it to iPhone Contacts.

Send it anywhere

Share your full card as a contact file by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or any app, or show it full screen with the brightness turned up.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your cards and contacts stay on your iPhone.

How it works

Ready in about a minute.

  1. Create your card

    Add your name, title, contact details, links, photo and logo, then choose a style.

  2. Show your code

    Open CardLinq, your widget or Apple Wallet and hold up your QR code.

  3. They’re saved

    Their camera reads your contact and offers to add it. Scan theirs back to save it in CardLinq.

Privacy

Your contacts are yours.

CardLinq works offline and has no accounts. We can’t see your cards or the people you meet.

Read the privacy policy →

  • Cards and contacts are stored only on your iPhone
  • No sign-up, ads, analytics or tracking
  • You choose which details each card shows
  • Wallet passes are signed and nothing is kept
  • Erase everything from Settings at any time
