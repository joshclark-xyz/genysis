Getting started

How do I create my card?

Open CardLinq and tap Create Your Card (or New Card on the My Cards tab). Fill in your name, title, company and contact details, add a photo and logo under Photos, and pick a theme, layout and font under Style. Tap Save.

Can I have more than one card?

Yes. Make a card for each role or business. On the My Cards tab, swipe between cards. To start a new card from an existing one, touch and hold the card and choose Duplicate.

How do I hide a detail without deleting it?

In the card editor, tap the eye next to a field. Hidden fields stay saved but don’t appear on your card, in your QR code or in the contact you share.

What are the networking sections?

Below Contact, you can fill in A Good Professional Connection for Me Is… and A Good Customer Referral for Me Is…. They show on your card and are included in the contact you share, so people know who to introduce you to.

Sharing your card

Does the other person need CardLinq?

No. Your QR code holds a standard contact card. Any iPhone or Android camera can scan it and offer to save you to their contacts.

How do I make my QR code easier to scan?

Tap Full Screen under your card. CardLinq turns the screen brightness up while the code is showing and puts it back when you close it. Hold the phone steady about 15–30 cm (6–12 in) from their camera.

Why isn’t my photo or bio in the QR code?

A QR code can only hold so much. To keep it quick to scan, the code includes your name, title, company, contact details, links and tagline. To send everything, including your photo, bio and networking sections, tap Share and send your card by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or any app.

The QR code looks very dense or won’t scan

Long websites, many links or long text make the code denser. Try hiding fields you don’t need on that card, shortening links, or using Share instead.

Home Screen widget

How do I add the QR widget?
  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit (or +) in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for CardLinq, choose small, medium or large, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Tap Done.
How do I choose which card the widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and pick a card. If you have several cards, add a widget for each one.

The widget says “Create a card in CardLinq”

Open CardLinq and make sure you’ve saved at least one card. The widget updates when you save or edit a card. If it still doesn’t update, remove the widget and add it again.

Apple Wallet

How do I add my card to Apple Wallet?

On the My Cards tab, swipe to the card and tap Add to Apple Wallet below it, then Add. Show it from the Wallet app, or double-click the side button to reach it from the Lock Screen.

I changed my card. Does the Wallet pass update?

Not automatically. After editing your card, tap Add to Apple Wallet again and the pass is replaced with the new version.

“Add to Apple Wallet” doesn’t work

Creating a pass needs an internet connection, because Apple requires each pass to be signed. Check you’re online and try again. Apple Wallet isn’t available on every device or in every region.

Scanning and saving contacts

How do I save someone else’s card?

Go to the Scan tab and point your camera at their QR code, or tap Scan from Photo to read a code from a photo or screenshot. Save them to CardLinq, or tap Add to iPhone Contacts.

The camera won’t open on the Scan tab

CardLinq needs permission to use the camera. Tap Open Settings on the Scan tab, or go to the Settings app › CardLinq and turn on Camera. You can still use Scan from Photo without camera access.

It says “This QR code contains text, not a contact”

That code doesn’t hold contact details. It might be a website or plain text. If it’s a web link, you can open it in Safari.

Can I import contacts from a file?

Yes. On the Contacts tab, tap Import vCard and choose a .vcf file. To take all your saved contacts elsewhere, go to Settings › Export All Contacts.

Your data

Do I need an account?

No. CardLinq has no accounts or sign-in. Everything is stored on your iPhone.

Is my data backed up?

Your cards and contacts are included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backup. Restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them back. If you delete CardLinq without a backup, its data can’t be recovered.

My cards disappeared after an update

Early test versions of CardLinq could hide cards after an update. Update to the latest version, then go to Settings › Recover Cards from Earlier Versions. CardLinq looks for cards from the earlier version and brings them back. If it says a file couldn’t be read, email us and we’ll help.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings › Erase All Data. This permanently removes all your cards and saved contacts from the iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. Remove any Wallet passes from the Wallet app.
