The methodology

CASPER

Six stages that take a business from owner-dependent to scalable. It is the framework behind the consulting practice — and the basis of the business-scaling services the distribution channel will offer.

C

Clarity

Find the real constraint before spending money on symptoms.

A

Architecture

Design roles, structure and decision flow deliberately.

S

Systems

Build the tools and management systems execution needs.

P

Processes

Make execution repeatable and documented.

E

Evaluation

Measure performance with practical KPIs.

R

Replication

Make success transferable and scalable.
