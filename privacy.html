1. Who we are

Genysis IQ ("Genysis IQ", "we", "us") is a business consultancy based in Orlando, Florida, providing business scaling, systems and process design, sales development, practical AI strategy and related services to clients across the United States.

This policy covers www.genysisiq.com and the advisor dashboard hosted on it. It does not cover any third-party website you reach by following a link from ours.

For anything in this policy, write to info@genysisiq.com or call 689.388.7353.

We only collect what we need to answer you and to run the services you sign up for.

NameSo we know who we are replying to.
CompanyOptional. Helps us prepare for the conversation.
Email addressRequired, so we can reply.
Phone numberOptional, if you would rather we called.
Your messageWhatever you choose to tell us.

When you call us

Our phone line is answered by an automated assistant at any hour. It may take your name, your reason for calling and appointment details so we can follow up. If you would rather not speak to an automated system, email us instead and a person will reply.

Technical information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers record standard server information such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request, which is used to deliver the site and to defend it against abuse. We do not combine this with anything else to build a profile of you.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and provide the services you have asked for
  • To create, secure and operate your dashboard account
  • To run the AI assistant configured for your company
  • To store and return the files you upload
  • To keep records we are required to keep, and to resolve disputes
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud and unlawful use

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or list builders, and we do not send marketing email to people who have only used the contact form to ask a question.

4. Artificial intelligence

Parts of this service are powered by large language models. When you send a message to an assistant, that message — together with recent messages in the same conversation and the instructions written for your company — is transmitted to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply.

Do not put secrets into a chat. Please do not send passwords, card numbers, government identifiers, protected health information or anything else you would not want leaving your building. An assistant is a drafting and thinking tool, not a secure vault.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It is not professional, legal, financial, medical or security advice, and it should be checked by a competent person before you rely on it. See our Terms of Service for more on this.

5. Files you upload

The File depot stores files in object storage operated by Cloudflare. Each company's files are held under a separate key space, and access is checked against your sign-in token on every request, so one client cannot reach another's files.

Uploading is disabled by default and only enabled at your request. Genysis IQ staff may access files where necessary to operate the service, provide support, or comply with the law. You are responsible for what you upload — the rules are set out in the terms you accept before your first upload and in our Terms of Service.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The advisor dashboard stores a sign-in token in your browser so you are not asked to sign in on every page, plus two small preferences: whether you have accepted the file upload terms, and whether you have chosen to be signed out when the browser closes. These are functional, stay on your device, and are never used to track you across other websites.

8. How long we keep it

  • Contact enquiries — kept while we are in touch and for a reasonable period afterwards as a record of the conversation.
  • Dashboard accounts — kept while the account is open. Ask us to close it and we delete the account and its profile.
  • Conversations — kept until you delete them. Deleting a conversation in the dashboard removes its messages too.
  • Files — kept until you delete them or the account is closed.
  • Records we must keep — retained for as long as the law requires, even after an account is closed.

9. Security

Traffic to this site is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Database access is restricted so an account can only read and write its own records, enforced by the database itself rather than only by the interface. Dashboard sessions sign out automatically after a period of inactivity, and you can choose to have them end as soon as the browser closes.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information sent over the internet will never be intercepted. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your account and the information tied to it
  • Send you a copy of your information
  • Stop sending you marketing messages

Email info@genysisiq.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify who you are first. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

California residents. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the past twelve months.

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, we process your information to perform a contract with you, to comply with the law, or on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and securing this service. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

This service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will make reasonable efforts to tell account holders directly rather than relying on you to notice.
