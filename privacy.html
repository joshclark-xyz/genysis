  • To store and return the files you upload

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or list builders, and we do not send marketing email to people who have only used the contact form to ask a question.

4. Artificial intelligence

Parts of this service are powered by large language models. When you send a message to an assistant, that message — together with recent messages in the same conversation and the instructions written for your company — is transmitted to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply.

Do not put secrets into a chat. Please do not send passwords, card numbers, government identifiers, protected health information or anything else you would not want leaving your building. An assistant is a drafting and thinking tool, not a secure vault.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It is not professional, legal, financial, medical or security advice, and it should be checked by a competent person before you rely on it. See our Terms of Service for more on this.

5. Files you upload

The File depot stores files in object storage operated by Cloudflare. Each company's files are held under a separate key space, and access is checked against your sign-in token on every request, so one client cannot reach another's files.

Uploading is disabled by default and only enabled at your request. Genysis IQ staff may access files where necessary to operate the service, provide support, or comply with the law. You are responsible for what you upload — the rules are set out in the terms you accept before your first upload and in our Terms of Service.

7. Cookies and measurement

This website uses no advertising cookies, no third-party analytics and no tracking pixels. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel and no advertising network on this site. There is no cookie banner because there are no consent-requiring cookies to accept.

What we do measure

We run our own traffic measurement, written to our own database, so we can see which pages are useful and where the site loses people. For each page you open we record the page address and title, the site that linked you here, whether you were on a phone, tablet or computer, how long the page was actually visible on your screen, how far down you scrolled, and which links, buttons and forms you clicked.

This measurement is deliberately built to identify pages, not people:

  • No IP address is stored.
  • No cookie is set — the identifier lives in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie sent to any server by default.
  • The identifier is a random number your own browser generates. It means nothing outside this website, is not linked to your name or email, and cannot be used to recognise you anywhere else.
  • No third party receives any of it. It goes only to Genysis IQ.
  • We never record what you type into a form field — only that a form was submitted.
Turning it off. Clearing your browser's site data for this website erases the identifier entirely and a new one is generated on your next visit. We also honour a permanent opt-out: email info@genysisiq.com and we will tell you how to set it, or clear the site data and use a private window.

Local storage we use

The advisor dashboard stores a sign-in token in your browser so you are not asked to sign in on every page, plus two small preferences: whether you have accepted the file upload terms, and whether you have chosen to be signed out when the browser closes. These are functional, stay on your device, and are never used to track you across other websites.

8. Phone calls and text messages

We do not sell or share your mobile number. Ever. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors who provide support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

When you call us

Our line, 689.388.7353, is answered 24 hours a day by an automated assistant. It identifies itself as automated. To help you and follow up afterwards, it may collect your name, your callback number, the reason for your call and any appointment details.

Calls may be recorded or transcribed. Florida is an all-party-consent state, so we tell you at the start of the call. You may decline recording and continue, ask for a person, or email us instead. Recordings and transcripts are used to service your request and to improve the assistant, are kept for up to 12 months, and are never sold.

Text messages

We only text a mobile number when its owner has given us express written consent — by ticking the box on our contact form, by asking us in writing, or by texting us first. Consent is never a condition of buying anything.

What we store when you opt in:

Mobile numberSo we can send the messages you asked for.
Record of your consentThe date, and how you gave it. We are required to be able to prove it.
Message historyWhat was sent and whether it was delivered.
Opt-out statusIf you reply STOP, we keep your number on a do-not-contact list so we do not text you again by mistake.

Messages are delivered through a licensed messaging provider acting on our instructions. They may process your number only to transmit our messages, and may not use it for anything else.

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. You can also email info@genysisiq.com or call us. The full messaging terms — programme description, frequency and rates — are in our Terms of Service.

9. How long we keep it

  • Contact enquiries — kept while we are in touch and for a reasonable period afterwards as a record of the conversation.
  • Dashboard accounts — kept while the account is open. Ask us to close it and we delete the account and its profile.
  • Conversations — kept until you delete them. Deleting a conversation in the dashboard removes its messages too.
  • Files — kept until you delete them or the account is closed.
  • Call recordings and transcripts — kept for up to 12 months.
  • Text messages — message history kept for up to 12 months. A record of your consent, and of any opt-out, is kept for as long as we are required to be able to prove it.
  • Traffic measurement — page view and click records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Records we must keep — retained for as long as the law requires, even after an account is closed.

10. Security

Traffic to this site is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Database access is restricted so an account can only read and write its own records, enforced by the database itself rather than only by the interface. Dashboard sessions sign out automatically after a period of inactivity, and you can choose to have them end as soon as the browser closes.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information sent over the internet will never be intercepted. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.
